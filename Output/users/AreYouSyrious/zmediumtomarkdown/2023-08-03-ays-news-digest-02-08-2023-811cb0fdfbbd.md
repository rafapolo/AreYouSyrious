### Twelve years after ECHR ruled Greece violates human rights — no improvements


![Justice for Pylos: protest outside the Greek Ministry of Migrations and Asylum. Photo Credit: [NoBorders](https://twitter.com/Refugees_Gr/status/1686411407892291584?fbclid=IwAR2HFug_sDkTOfs9bXUqSSHmdO90A1gDtVGcdJRbo_4XdaV4YNvQ4-WVfsI)](assets/811cb0fdfbbd/0*P7uXrnuj8HCyQ-FF)

Justice for Pylos: protest outside the Greek Ministry of Migrations and Asylum. Photo Credit: [NoBorders](https://twitter.com/Refugees_Gr/status/1686411407892291584?fbclid=IwAR2HFug_sDkTOfs9bXUqSSHmdO90A1gDtVGcdJRbo_4XdaV4YNvQ4-WVfsI)

2011 saw the European Court of Human Rights rule that the Greek government was in violation of Article 3 of the European Convention of Human Rights. A new report looks at the current situation 12 years on.

[Lack of sufficient accommodation and asylum procedures are highlighted](https://www.mobileinfoteam.org/mss-submission?fbclid=IwAR1SyEBW8T_Z7TcXrPlHbkUj1B9PjINRSO_XmLQs3MZtANTzDpjwwueNTOA) as remaining woefully insufficient in comparison with 12 years ago.


> Article 3 protects you from: 





> torture (mental or physical) inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment, and deportation or extradition (being sent to another country to face criminal charges) if there is a real risk you will face torture or inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment in the country concerned. 





This led to a ban on deportations from within the EU to Greece under the Dublin Regulation. To be “Dublin-ed” means that an asylum seeker would be returned to the first EU country where there is a record of them requesting asylum. In reality this isn’t a straightforward procedure — [in 2017, Hungary attempted to return 4,000 people to Greece with only a handful of people actually being returned](https://www.fes.de/en/displacement-migration-integration/article-page-flight-migration-integration/what-return-to-dublin-means-for-greece) .

This new report is penned by two groups working every day with people-on-the-move in Greece Mobile Info Team and I Have Rights. They detail the severe problems that still remain which are in violation of Article 3, offering changes that need to be made referring to the asylum procedure at various steps as well as the living conditions that people waiting are forced to live in.

In theory, the [revised Dublin III regulation](https://help.unhcr.org/greece/applying-for-asylum/dublin-iii-family-reunification-and-other-legal-pathways/) is supposed to ease the burden on those countries at the EU’s border by transferring people legally to other Dublin III countries. For example, an unaccompanied minor would be transferred from Greece to Germany if they had family already in Germany. In practice the wheels of bureaucracy grind very slowly and it seems that the regulation doesn’t often work in favour of those at it’s mercy.

The report in question offers improvements that would potentially allow deportations to Greece to resume — yet it seems unlikely that Greece will willingly make those changes, giving itself an even greater burden should returns resume. Meanwhile, the reality for people bound by this regulation is often to wait in temporary accommodation in another country until the valid months of Dublin III have expired — anyone with experience of the asylum system in Europe will know the mental health impact of constant waiting.

[The full report can be read here.](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/597473fe9de4bb2cc35c376a/t/64c8d54540f8ad4d37147e08/1690883400252/MIT-Final.pdf?fbclid=IwAR3Do9yVBiw2anxGvpnZaAQdlak_X-6U57TWVDN32_PoiHz_egLG1Zjmg-k)
#### SEA and SAR

As of the end of June, 539 boats reached out for support in the central Mediterranean Sea this year. [Alarm Phone have published their half-yearly report](https://alarmphone.org/en/2023/07/31/continuous-landings-increasing-violence-and-deaths-at-sea/?fbclid=IwAR3UZDs53bdI8mLVS3pWMwPit3RH5k6D0rwAEfSW5DZdlLwNROojCtl0hu0) which also indicates that militarisation of the sea and African borders isn’t working, with a record 65,000 people being documented as reaching Italy. The report also examines changes to where boats depart from in Egypt and Libya as well as Malta’s lack of participation in rescues despite maritime law.

#### HUNGARY
#### Child death in Hungary car crash

#### LATVIA
#### Legal action by asylum seekers taken to ECHR

A man from Guinea who faced human rights abuses both at the border with Latvia and within Latvian detention, is [taking the case to the European Court of Human Rights](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2023/08/01/asylum-seekers-who-claimed-torture-and-abuse-in-latvia-are-using-the-courts-to-fight-back?fbclid=IwAR0bf34ESJPjQN-jSoQKNIGK7Rkvf6uom52oP39iizpvn4wpwbaAu6cex3U) . The 20-year-old was trapped at the border between Belarus and Latvia for six months, where he was physically and verbally abused and denied access to food. He was in closed detention in Latvia for eight months before Doctors Without Borders along with a local human rights group helped in his release.

Two unsuccessful cases have been filed against the State regarding this individual’s inhumane treatment, so with the help of lawyers from the group Respect, Protect, Fulfil (RPF), the case has now been escalated to ECHR. Lawyers from RPF claim that more than 100 other people may have similar cases against authorities, although claims at the national level are likely to be unsuccessful, as Latvia changed laws allowing use of force at the borders.

[Background to the Belarus situation can be found in this News Digest.](ays-new-digest-22-8-22-pushback-after-pushback-after-pushback-afea0082ac2e)
#### SPAIN
#### Authorities failed to help overturned boat, Melilla

A boat carrying 11 people was trying to reach Melilla from mainland Morocco when it collided with the boat of the Civil Guard and overturned. The passengers, which included a child and a baby, were then rescued from the water.

The incident has sparked anger among groups at the tone of reporting in the press as well as the actions of the Civil Guard. [This video shows the chaotic rescue](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmQiKvd4u_U) that ensued, involving members of the public. The reporting in [Europa Press](https://www-europapress-es.translate.goog/ceuta-y-melilla/noticia-rescatados-once-migrantes-patera-melilla-embestir-geas-volcar-20230731221339.html?_x_tr_sl=es&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=en-US&_x_tr_pto=wapp) implies that the boat deliberately collided with that of the Civil Guard — the rhetoric surrounding migration as well as the power of the press is the target for solidarity groups.

#### ITALY
#### Iuventa vessel — seized six years ago

In 2017 the rescue vessel Iuventa was seized by Italian authorities. In one year the ship had rescued 14,000 people. The boat was confiscated on suspicion of “criminal activities” and cases were brought against the crew. Six of the ten crew have had the charges dropped but [four are still awaiting a judge’s decision.](https://iuventa-crew.org/en/) Meanwhile, the ship rots in the harbour of Trapani.

#### FRANCE

A man from Cameroon is being repeatedly denied asylum in France. Hervé’s personal story is harrowing. He lost his wife and child on the boat crossing the Mediterranean and then his best friend when authorities pursued them on the French-Italian border. Hervé then risked his own safety to testify in the case being brought against authorities and is now facing deportation. [This petition is asking the Prefecture of Rhône to support him staying in France.](https://www.change.org/p/pour-la-regularisation-d-herve-s-principal-temoin-de-la-mort-de-blessing-matthew?recruiter=1150808567&recruited_by_id=ab55f2a0-fc19-11ea-8ba3-639372391723&utm_source=share_petition&utm_campaign=share_petition&utm_term=share_petition&utm_medium=email&fbclid=IwAR30ECJrNsceU18AAQb89ahbM9KnrKQfmTymVo9fcfowjG6Sdbt7q8tHdi0)
