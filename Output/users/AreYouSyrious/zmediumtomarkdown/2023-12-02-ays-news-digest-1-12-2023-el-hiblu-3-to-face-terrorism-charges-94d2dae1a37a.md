### El Hiblu three to face terrorism charges


![Photo Credit Amnesty International/Joanna Demarco](assets/94d2dae1a37a/0*6_sU8orB81PQzadT)

Photo Credit Amnesty International/Joanna Demarco

Three teenagers took their fate into their own hands in 2019. Four and a half years later, they face the most drastic charges possible.

Amara Kromah, Abdul Kader and Abdalla Bari took control of the oil tanker El Hiblu 1 when it became clear that the crew wanted to return them, illegally, to Libya. The lads, who were aged 15, 16 and 19 at the time, were arrested upon arrival in Malta and have been waiting ever since to hear what charges they will be tried for.

Amnesty International describes the decision to charge the young men from West Africa as “the worst possible decision”.


> “The indictment fails to recognize that they were part of a group of more than 100 asylum-seekers faced with an illegal pushback to Libya which would have put their lives at risk. Yet, they are the ones who might now need to defend themselves against charges ranging from ‘acts of terrorism’ to ‘violence’.” [Elisa De Pieri, Amnesty International’s Regional Researcher.](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2023/11/malta-indictment-of-el-hiblu-3-the-worst-possible-decision/?fbclid=IwAR1dfePntteetH5zN4UEfjpMI_rUErNh1hDfI0yyDZ_VYzZp93brK1FFf_o) 




The charges for terrorism could lead to a life sentence. The legal team, support by the [Aditus Foundation](https://twitter.com/aditusNGO/status/1730111099922452924?fbclid=IwAR0HIiHhkJ6MAHSPEXVHKaXMVR7d_VpkQ6rqblke2D3v6uzMNplHpCrPEWY) , will continue to support the group, although Kader has been [missing since summer](https://timesofmalta.com/articles/view/one-el-hiblu-three-missing-since-early-august-court-told.1056214) and is believed to have left [the island of Malta.](https://timesofmalta.com/articles/view/el-hiblu-3-face-terrorism-charges-ag-issues-bill-indictment.1070316?fbclid=IwAR04SKFbQosc8_r3iesdvx1fnuNZLY89xG0QeMfYQxtP0Az07vrVbeUldLg)

The reaction of the NGO community has been strong, and Are You Syrious adds its voice to those denouncing the decision and calling for justice to be served. A petition has been started to gather support from around the world. Y [ou can find the petition here.](https://www.change.org/p/drop-the-charges-against-the-el-hiblu-3)

#### FRANCE
#### Huge eviction in winter conditions

Multiple sites have been cleared in Northern France. In what is one of the biggest operations in recent history, at [least three sites in Calais](https://twitter.com/Care4Calais/status/1730274011005735018) were cleared of tents and personal belongings, whilst trees and shrubs and anything that could offer shelter were also removed, with Grand-Synthe, Dunkirk also being affected. People were forced onto buses and taken to other locations in France against their will.
#### NETHERLANDS
#### 24 week work maximum unlawful

Until now, authorities in the Netherlands imposed a maximum number of working weeks that someone seeking asylum could work. [Working for only 24 out of 52 weeks has been ruled unlawful](https://www.raadvanstate.nl/actueel/nieuws/november/24-weken-eis-is-onverbindend/) . The European Reception Directive requires European Union member states to allow asylum seekers to have effective access to the labour market, but Member States are allowed to set their own terms. The challenge to the rules was made by an employer when they had not been allowed a work permit for two employees.
