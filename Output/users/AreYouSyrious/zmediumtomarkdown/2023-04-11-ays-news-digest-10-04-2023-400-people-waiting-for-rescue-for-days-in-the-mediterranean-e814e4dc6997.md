#### SEA/SAR
### More deaths in the Mediterranean sea

Over the past few days more deaths have been counted following shipwrecks in various parts of the Mediterranean.

One almost loses count: two shipwrecks overnight on Friday 7th April near the Tunisian coast; another near Lampedusa, on Saturday 8th April.


![[Credit: Resqship](https://twitter.com/resqship_int/status/1644992867759276032)](assets/e814e4dc6997/1*aefsg2WYd4FqEVMydiTKhA.jpeg)

[Credit: Resqship](https://twitter.com/resqship_int/status/1644992867759276032)

More and more deaths. People. People left to die at the borders.

According to [ResQship](https://twitter.com/resqship_int/status/1644992867759276032) at least 23 people died after the shipwreck close to the Lampedusa coast, but they were able to rescue 22 people, now safe in Lampedusa.

At least [20 people are missing](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/48087/migrant-boats-sink-off-tunisia-more-than-20-missing-4-dead?fbclid=IwAR2VKzNEOITRv24EeRFCk7foPlvhK2I1rhWLrBARJErvQ3S-rei87aA4vtQ) following the shipwrecks on Friday and Saturday near the Tunisian town of Sfax, while at least four have died.


[![](https://static.dw.com/image/65265551_6.jpg)](https://www.dw.com/en/migrant-boats-sink-off-tunisia-more-than-20-missing-4-dead/a-65265592?maca=en-rss-en-all-1573-rdf&fbclid=IwAR2tqWSUTljXIKIZQ1mGSow7LA_8LLcxjaonyU_pyfrWlW36c_xQQDbyaVQ)


Most of them, according to sources, appear to be sub-Saharan people: the situation in Tunisia for people from that area is getting worse by the day due to increasingly explosive hatred and racism. Those who decide to leave the country find themselves risking their lives, having no other choice. The Tunisian situation is just one of many. Those who cross the Mediterranean choose this route because they do not have the possibility to take other regular and safe ones. And so people are left to die.

For days, 400 people have been waiting for rescue from the Maltese authorities, who seem to ignore every alarm raised even by the NGOs

With the hours passing and no rescue from Malta, the boat has approached the Italian coast. At the moment it has not yet received rescue, according to [AlarmPhone](https://twitter.com/alarm_phone/status/1645722040094056448) .

Meanwhile, according to the Italian authorities, at least 1,200 people were rescued over the past days.
#### TUNISIA
### Violence towards sub-Saharan people in Tunisia is increasing by the day

In recent months, President Kais Saied has fuelled hate speech towards the migrant population, justifying and inciting violent acts against them.

UN Committee on the Elimination of Racial Discrimination (CERD) appealed to the Tunisian authorities to end “hate speech and violence against migrants from south of the Sahara”.

#### ITALY
### Reportedly poor conditions in a reception centre

Few beds, infested with insects and only one meal a day. These are the conditions in which the people living in the reception centre in Liguria live. The co-operative who manages the centre has become ‘infamous for mismanagement’, with the workers, who are underpaid, gathering to protest and highlight the situation of the workers and residents of the centre.

[Read more here](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/48079/dire-condition-in-migrant-hosting-centers-in-genoa?fbclid=IwAR1pjYpnMm7ZxW2vTmNS6eK31vWYmMoqAVQ5ZjSrmlgJDKZlY0z2Kcev8MA)
#### POLAND
### A man who fell from the border fence between Poland and Belarus is in critical condition in hospital


> “In one of the hospitals in the forest lies an unconscious and intubated man from Syria, who had fallen off the border fence in recent days. He has multiple internal injuries, both legs broken, a punctured artery.”
