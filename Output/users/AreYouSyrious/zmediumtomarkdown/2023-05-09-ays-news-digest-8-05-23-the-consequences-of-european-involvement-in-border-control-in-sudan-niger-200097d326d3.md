#### NIGER
### Border management shows its lethality in Niger

Six people on the move had an accident while trying to reach Libya from Niger. They were rescued by Alarmphone Sahara, but this is further proof of the dangerous route that many people are forced to take because of the increasing border controls.

> > Rescue in Latai (Northern #Niger), 3/5/23: 30km from Latai on the Agadez-Dirkou axis there was an accident with a vehicle carrying internal migrants on board on their way to #Libya. APS team rescued them and informed @[MSF_WestAfrica](https://twitter.com/MSF_WestAfrica). 6 people were injured + evacuated to #Agadez. https://t.co/sD5OS14x3P

■■■■■■■■■■■■■■ 


Since 2015, new forms of border control have been established in Niger with the financial and technical support of EU institutions and member states.

The deadly effects of these border practices are highlighted and analysed in a recently published Border Forensics investigation ( [here is the full report](https://www.borderforensics.org/app/uploads/2023/05/Report_Sahara_EN.pdf) ).

[**Mission Accomplished? The Deadly Effects Of Border Control In Niger**](https://www.borderforensics.org/investigations/niger-investigation/?fbclid=IwAR3k8uaXxniNPwKAQkp-v047wmwC40Sz5MPa4gxwGnM12mNY8YnpGr-s1CU) 
[_On May 26, 2015, the Parliament of Niger adopted Law No. 2015-36 on Illegal Trafficking of Migrants, which resulted in…_ www.borderforensics.org](https://www.borderforensics.org/investigations/niger-investigation/?fbclid=IwAR3k8uaXxniNPwKAQkp-v047wmwC40Sz5MPa4gxwGnM12mNY8YnpGr-s1CU)

Since Niger adopted Law №2015–36 on the Illegal Trafficking of Migrants, the approach to migration management has been repressive and security-based. Border Forensics’s report states:


> “In the years that have followed, thousands of incidents of migrant deaths and disappearances have been recorded in northern Niger. Transport drivers — now characterized as negligent and greedy smugglers — were blamed for the increase in deaths and disappearances by the Nigerien government and news media, as well EU agencies such as **Frontex** ” 





Over past years activists, journalists, and researchers have denounced the humanitarian consequences of this approach, while Niger was clearly becoming a strategic location of mobility control for the EU management of border externalization. However, it has been very diffult to obtain data on the deaths in that area. In their investigation, Border Forensics analysed the lethal effects of Law 2015–36 thanks to geo-spatial analysis and remote sensing methodologies.


> “We demonstrate how this dispersal sees migrants’ trajectories move deeper into the desert, where chances of survival are greatly diminished in the recurring events of vehicle failures, abandonment, or passengers running out of water (…) This evidence may support calls for increased accountability of all actors participating in border management, in particular the Nigerien government, the EU, and its Member States, as well as UN agencies”

#### SUDAN
### European responsibilities in the Sudanese conflict

Sea-Watch pointed out the responsibility of Europe in the Sudan conflict: through the Emergency Trust Fund, the EU supported the country with means and funds in order to monitor borders and migration routes in the area.

The funds for different types of border control technologies have restricted the freedom of movement of people, while at the same time giving the Sudanese government more control capacities over its citizens.

> > Mit dem Emergency Trust Fund verspricht die EU Unterstützung im Sudan. Die Mittel und Gelder fließen dabei in die Überwachung von Migrationsrouten. Von 2015 bis 2019 finanzierte die EU damit maßgeblich Menschenhandel. Die Folgen bleiben bis heute spürbar. [Ein Thread] https://t.co/Y3CFbFaH79 

> **Tweeted at [2023-05-03 16:14:56](https://twitter.com/seawatchcrew/status/1653795284495859712?s=20).** 

■■■■■■■■■■■■■■ 



> “In particular, border control by the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) was financed with the [EU Trust Funds](https://trust-fund-for-africa.europa.eu/index_en) . RSF soldiers are often ex-militia, retrained to monitor migration routes // The former militias (RFS) play a dual role. Officially, they are the most important “border troops”. At the same time, they are themselves involved in human trafficking and earn money by smuggling people” 





as Sea-Watch explained.

[Here](https://www.fmreview.org/externalisation/lumleysapanski-schwarz-valverdecano) is an essay with a deeper explanation of the EU borders externalisation in Sudan, where the conflict has already claimed many victims ( [here](https://antira.org/2023/05/08/sudan-konflikt-eskaliert-nazisymbole-verboten-amokfahrer-verurteilt/?fbclid=IwAR0AnKzlomeL8ZaJj0yTw1ak3btL3xiE1YMZVR-LoJAt4rT5l6-jiTFWw9U#Bereits_1008217000_Menschen_flohen_vor_Krieg_im_Sudan) are updates on the developments in Sudan).

As explained in the mentioned essay:


> “The Emergency Trust Fund for Africa (EUTF) contributed an initial €40 million to the Khartoum Process for 2016–19. These funds are distributed between policy harmonisation (12.5%), capacity building (62.5%), protection (17.5%) and awareness raising (7.6%). The majority of money allocated to capacity building went to law enforcement, justice and border security “ 





EU interventions and funding in Sudan have contributed to the current situation, which the country is experiencing so dramatically. In this way, the EU is also responsible for the [flight](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/48614/sudan-updates-un-says-conflict-may-trigger-refugee-crisis?fbclid=IwAR1kvGjwIIy-5GYxCIpoHxaGJXnDalGwPaCBd99wEJLs66flQkEK_1_lbPI) from the country of more and more people trying to save their lives.
#### SEA/SAR
### 170 people at risk of capsizing are waiting for help

Last Sunday, 130 people in distress were rescued by the [Nadir crew](https://twitter.com/alarm_phone/status/1655442354747187200?s=20&fbclid=IwAR0znwnFjMghtZrBrdPJgxVOdNvCSv1UCT63GZsq6PTig81y5SUl_cSObhs) after having been on the open sea for three days.


![Credit: [Resqship](https://twitter.com/resqship_int/status/1655155558402387968)](assets/200097d326d3/1*kQIGLP24Dtv9VcN2zDFoNg.jpeg)

Credit: [Resqship](https://twitter.com/resqship_int/status/1655155558402387968)

However [it wasn’t the only boat](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/48752/three-dead-and-234-rescued-in-central-mediterranean?fbclid=IwAR17oxGmyeyUxdTC1cH0XUpIEoh-a4V0ET7GBWfj0RKDh10MxrmpTis2I8w) waiting to be rescued. According to Seebrücke, in total 234 people last Sunday were saved; but three people lost their lives. They were then brought to the Italian island of Lampedusa.

The Resqship crew explained that:


> “Since the beginning of the year, we have counted at least 850 deaths and missing in the Mediterranean” 





Moreover many people in distress, at risk of capsizing, are still waiting for help. The European authorities have been alerted, but they have not yet responded to the alarm.

#### POLAND-BELARUS
### An injured person stranded for days at the Polish-Belarus border

He waited in the cold, in the rain, in the forest. A 31-year-old person on the move has been lying in the mud with an injured leg after being pushed back at the EU [border](https://twitter.com/kapturak/status/1655216951180898304?s=20&fbclid=IwAR3YWZByrRVWZRJqK0PZ6ltJI9PH3YwPnHwitXRevLGYIa2nEJG3ggYqqdI) .

It is a situation that has been going on already for more than one year. The fence between Belarus and Poland is putting lives at risk and causing many serious injuries. Over the past months, many people have died while crossing the border and seeking safety. We share a part of No Border Team’s statement:


> After the winter, the number of people at the border who need help has increased dramatically (…) So far, over 40 bodies of people who sought refuge in Europe have been found on the Polish side of the wall. We also know about several missing people who are still searched for by relatives. From the people we meet, we get information about many deaths on the Belarusian side, bodies lying in the forest.. but we are unable to confirm it. Large groups of migrants camped on the Belarusian side cannot turn back into Belarus because they are guarded by army troops. People on the road, whom we meet in the forest, report cases of violence by Belarusian and Polish border guards. Spring has come, but it does not mean an easier way for people in the forest — the temperature drops below zero at night, many people are soaked and chilled. Some have wounds caused by injuries when crossing the barrier — sprains of limbs and deep cuts with razor wire (..) 




#### GERMANY
### Meeting in Germany to discuss the financial costs of migration policies on 10th May

The externalisation of Europe’s borders is the main path followed by the European authorities over the past several years. Germany’s coalition government is also pushing in this direction, as [we already wrote](ays-news-digest-06-05-2023-greek-police-shoot-an-unarmed-man-in-the-head-no-one-reports-anything-1b3b90839376) . In fact, on 10th May, the German chancellery will meet to discuss the financial costs of migration policies: it is clearly their intention to finance border controls at EU borders in order to limit the numbers of people entering Europe.
