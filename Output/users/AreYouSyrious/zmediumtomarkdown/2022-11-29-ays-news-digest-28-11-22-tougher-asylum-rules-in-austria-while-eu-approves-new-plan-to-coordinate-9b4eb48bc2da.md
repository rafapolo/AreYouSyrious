#### FEATURE
### Tensions between European countries over migration topic lead to a new plan with ‘little new’


> The new plan would see Brussels work more closely with Tunisia, Libya and Egypt to try to stop undocumented migrants boarding smuggler vessels in the first place 





This is what the ‘new’ migration plan approved by the EU in recent days seems to have led to: a further step in the process of externalising European borders. The words above, quoted from [this article](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20221126-eu-ministers-endorse-new-migrant-plan-after-france-italy-tensions?fbclid=IwAR1nUL3BvWW4Buk8pnd5e3fy40LsCy2UFBNjPTvnP1GCgv19Rz9_dI_fPx0) , are proof of this. Europe continues to stand by and, indeed, collaborate with the atrocities against migrants in Libya while the French and Italian governments bicker amongst themselves. Meanwhile, people die. They die at sea, they die waiting to disembark, they die because Europe only shows walls even on arrival. Read [more here](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20221126-eu-ministers-endorse-new-migrant-plan-after-france-italy-tensions?fbclid=IwAR1nUL3BvWW4Buk8pnd5e3fy40LsCy2UFBNjPTvnP1GCgv19Rz9_dI_fPx0) .

France accused Italy of failing to respect the law of the sea by rejecting an NGO ship earlier this month, triggering crisis talks in Brussels. The meetings resulted in the new plan of sharing responsibilities between EU countries. The plan doesn’t really show anything new, but calmed down the tensions between the two countries. A new meeting will be held on 8th December. Read more here:


[![](https://gdb.voanews.com/09410000-0a00-0242-276d-08dacf559f24_cx0_cy7_cw0_w1200_h630.jpg)](https://www.voanews.com/a/eu-ministers-endorse-new-migrant-plan-after-france-italy-spat-/6850975.html?fbclid=IwAR2tZQvFM0MWNdvhjns4176hRjB13z3vRYY14aybYDah6m7ds9wkc-wuq4Q)


All participants stated that “more can and must be done”.. not a new sentence. The direction seems to be always the same: externalisation and securitisation.

The plan released on Monday by the European Commission unveiled a new action plan to better regulate arrivals on the central Mediterranean Sea route.

In the meantime, what worries many of the European countries is above all the land route. This is demonstrated by the actions taken by Austria, which to safeguard itself against ‘the threat to public order’ has tightened border controls and implemented new asylum policies. The Austrian parliament approved those tough new asylum measures on Wednesday, including a procedure that allows migrants to be turned back at the border within one hour. #### LIBYA
### Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court (ICC) requests arrest warrants against people involved in crimes against migrants in Libya

Karim Ahmad Khan (Prosecutor of the ICC) is seeking a number of arrest warrants against Libyan nationals over war crimes, crimes against migrants and crimes against human rights.

As reported in the Italian newspaper Avvenire, if the warrant were issued…


> “…many governments — including Italy, Malta, France, Turkey and Russia — will find themselves more than embarrassed to cooperate in the capture of characters with whom they have exchanged many handshakes”

#### GREECE
### Court hearing this Friday on NGO registry in Greece

The NGO registry seems to follow procedures that arbitrarily exclude some organisations and criminalising them. [Refugee Support Aegean](https://rsaegean.org/en/asylum-and-the-rule-of-law-greece/) (RSA) claimed:


> “The NGO Registry of the Ministry of Migration and Asylum is not implemented in a fair, transparent and consistent manner. 115 organisations have had their applications rejected. In RSA’s case, registration was arbitrarily rejected on the ground that provision of assistance to persons under deportation contravenes Greek law”

### Push-backs from Greece

[A video of refugees](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=3363547177299163) , including women and children, crossing from Turkey to Greece has been circulating on social media. They are subjected to violence by Greek police wearing black glasses and are pushed back into the Evros river.

Push-backs are a common practice carried out by Greek authorities on Greece’s different borders. [Last Thursday](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/44979/aegean-sea-68-migrants-rescued-after-alleged-pushback?fbclid=IwAR1TlWMoE-fgFiIzYwa1EszW90OCmVHOrDJ_AhYw2mzOmEx3yE-G02KJ9X0) 68 people were found by the Turkish coastguard in the Aegean sea: the people most likely having been pushed back by Greek police.

In parallel with the illegal pushback policy, Greek authorities focus on counter smuggling operations. Seven people were arrested last week accused of being the smugglers of 500 people who arrived in Crete. [Read more here](https://apnews.com/article/europe-middle-east-africa-egypt-arrests-1444550888be9a17142fb812adc7a189?fbclid=IwAR2z2IM2XRvex-nP-8ZmB_O-nwPVeaq4jjLsAnCIG42KZqRKoMMBTXEq3XI)

> “They \[Belgian officials\] haven’t given us anything to camp in and we don’t have shelter. We are facing problems with clothes and wearables and every authority we go to, they tell us that there is no place.” 





As shared by an Afghan asylum seeker (read more below). The man had been sleeping on the street due to the crisis reception that Belgium is going through. The European Court of Human Rights (ECHR) applies interim measures for the second time in the last few weeks requiring the Belgian authorities to provide accommodation to asylum seekers left without shelter.

#### UK
### Home Office changes the birth dates of asylum seeker minors

[A minor was attacked](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2022/nov/27/child-asylum-seekers-detained-as-adults-after-uk-home-office-alters-birth-dates?ref=upstract.com&fbclid=IwAR3o1LeUQX7g8s66_Hll2sM38uwdzVj9uHdPeyms3oxicMzkZCi8JXDNn80) by some adults in Manston, the migration centre in Kent. The facility is overcrowded and the poor conditions were denounced over the past weeks by many organisations. Tensions increase and with them more accidents happen inside the structure as a result of wider politics and the management of the migration system. In fact the minor shouldn’t have been in Manston, but he was considered by the Home Office as an adult. Changing the dates of birth of unaccompanied asylum seekers is becoming a very frequent practice: many minors are wrongly sent to the Manston centre by the Home Office.


![Coaches arriving to Manston migration centre. Via [SOAS Detainee Support](https://twitter.com/sdetsup/status/1597264486481555456?fbclid=IwAR17ffX9CdZsCVq7CxzoeNgD2zw88AaIRMkrs7kcpNFLKipVhhxt8LlLwRk)](assets/9b4eb48bc2da/1*AfzV7NFDfhRM8sHCwk1hKA.jpeg)
