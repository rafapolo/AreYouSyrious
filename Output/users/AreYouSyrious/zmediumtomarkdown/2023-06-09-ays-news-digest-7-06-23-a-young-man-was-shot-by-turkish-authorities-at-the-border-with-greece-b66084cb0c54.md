#### GREECE
### A report on the barriers of the a **sylum procedures on mainland Greece, Crete and Rhodes**

Greek authorities ignore international law to dehumanise people on the move, deny them their fundamental rights, and actively abuse them. This has emerged from the latest report _Blocked from the System_ released by Migrant Info Team (MIT), part of the Border Violence Monitoring Network (BVMN) collective.

_Blocked from the System_ describes the experiences of people have have been seeking asylum on mainland Greece, Crete and Rhodes since the policy changes in late November 2021, highlighting significant barriers that people face in attempting to register their asylum claims.


> “Without documents, people have no choice but to live on the fringes of society. With a fundamental lack of information, access or uniformity, this procedure is not compatible with the requirements of a fair and efficient system. Instead, it creates a hostile environment that forces people to live in dangerous and undignified situations. This puts people who are in desperate need of safety at further risk of severe physical and psychological harm”

#### LITHUANIA
### Detention of asylum seekers declared illegal by Lithuania’s Constitutional Court


> The declaration of a state of emergency is not in itself a ground for restricting the liberty of a person (…) ; the liberty of a person may be subject to limitation in order to protect the constitutional order of the state, as well as for the purposes of ensuring public order, the protection of the state, and state security. However, even when pursuing these objectives, the liberty of a person must not be limited or restricted on the basis of general grounds, without an assessment of the real threat posed by the person to the values protected by the Constitution where that threat would require the limitation of the person’s liberty 





The European Court of Justice already condemned arbitrary detentions of asylum seekers in Lithuania last year, but now (as written in the above mentioned text) Lithuania’s Constitutional Court’s judgement also considered those detentions illegal.

### Traumatic events in Calais make life hard for refugees


> “A young Sudanese man was killed when he fell under a lorry’s wheels, just a few days after another Sudanese refugee died after being hit by traffic. A mass eviction in Calais left hundreds without shelter and spare clothes, this coming just two weeks after another huge eviction in Dunkirk” 





Care4Calais denounced those events and underlined the importance of the activities in Calais in order to support people who feel every day refused by the institutions and European authorities.

However the aid is also criminalised, as Care4Calais explained:


> “the authorities ploughed up one of our main distribution sites, making it difficult for us to get essentials to people” 





Supporting asylum seeker, refugees and people on the move is a crucial political act when so many traumatic events make their lives impossible.

### PRAB Report on pushbacks at European borders


> “While Frontex claims that the number of irregular arrivals is again at pre-pandemic (COVID) high levels, this report clarifies the other side of the coin: that rights violations are part of Europe’s ‘welcome treatment’ for those being pushed back and — unfortunately — also at pre-pandemic levels” 




The 6th report from Protecting Rights At Borders (PRAB) documents extensive abuses, including pushbacks, systemic violence, double standards and increasing impunity for various European Border Agency. Here are some data released in the report:


> Denial of access to asylum procedures was reported to PRAB partners in 44% of all pushback cases recorded at the border between Croatia and Bosnia & Herzegovina, as well as in 88% of the cases recorded at the border between Hungary and Serbia, and in 85% of those at the border between Italy and France.

### On 8th June, the appeal trial of the Paros3 takes place

After a 439 YEAR-sentence, three men in search of safety were accused of smuggling. On 8th June the appeal trial takes place. They risk so many years in jail because they steered the boat on which they tried to reach the EU.


> “The appeal trial against Abdallah, Kheiraldin and Mohamad, known as the #Paros3, will take place on the Greek island of Syros. Because they had steered the boat on which they and around 80 other people tried to reach Europe, they were sentenced to a total of 439 years for smuggling on 5 June 2022. They have already spent 1.5 years in prison”
