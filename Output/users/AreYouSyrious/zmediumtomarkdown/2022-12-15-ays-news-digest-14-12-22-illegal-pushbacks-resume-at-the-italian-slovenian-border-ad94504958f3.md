### FEATURE
### Giorgia Meloni’s far-right government reactivates illegal pushbacks to Slovenia, calling them ‘informal readmissions’


![People on the move arriving from Slovenian border are often in poor health and are given help by the Linea d’Ombra association in Trieste. Credit: [Lorena Fornasir](https://www.facebook.com/lorena.fornasir/photos_by)](assets/ad94504958f3/1*oro0czjVC01K17ucESQ7Vw.jpeg)

People on the move arriving from Slovenian border are often in poor health and are given help by the Linea d’Ombra association in Trieste. Credit: [Lorena Fornasir](https://www.facebook.com/lorena.fornasir/photos_by)

In the square in front of the Trieste train station someone who came from the Balkan Route sits down every day to restore and to heal their aching feet. Broken shoes, injured feet, bruises are frequent among the photos of the Linea d’ombra association that helps those arriving in the city of Trieste in poor health. After such a long, risky journey, people on the move reaching Italy fear again to be pushed back to Slovenia. In fact, the far-right Giorgia Meloni government recently declared that ‘informal readmission’ to Slovenia will be reactivated.

Through an agreement with the Slovenian authorities, a practice declared illegal by the Court of Rome in January 2021 would thus be legalised once again. In 2020, rejections to Slovenia had become a systematic practice, but this was interrupted by the court declaration following a complaint by a Pakistani citizen.

Now people on the move will be ‘readmitted’ to Slovenia despite the fact that numerous reports and investigations (only recently a Lighthouse Report investigation found [secret facilities](https://www.lighthousereports.nl/investigation/europes-black-sites/) along the route where people are tortured) have spotlighted the human rights violations perpetrated by EU authorities such as Frontex, as well as by the authorities of the countries crossed along the journey.

Undersecretary for the Interior Emanuele Prisco stated ( [read more here](https://it.euronews.com/2022/12/06/migranti-prisco-in-vigore-riammissioni-con-slovenia?fbclid=IwAR3GUAY7AfxxE04w2bgYj9763rJQdWJUi9Kpaw42TK7Al4HmC7i1oDJP2I8) ):


> “Minister Piantedosi has issued a directive to the Border Police and Prefects
 

> to reuse the readmission mechanisms already considered by the Italian-Slovenian agreements”. 





This practice would be the starting point for chain pushbacks, which are illegal.


> “Article 3 of the European Convention on Human Rights and Article 4 of the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union, standards which require states not to return someone to a context where they risk being subjected to inhumane and degrading treatment. And this in view of the violence that existed along the Balkan route and to which these people were returned”, 





said Caterina Bove, [ASGI](https://www.asgi.it/) (Association for Juridical Studies on Immigration) lawyer, in a press conference available at [this link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kt6xDZvnkWE) .

Read more [here](https://altreconomia.it/sulla-sconcertante-ripresa-delle-riammissioni-informali-al-confine-italo-sloveno/?fbclid=IwAR3JBPqKhMCdTgo6AvxUDeEr-2A5X9-o6sw8cXYgnas5Lcs-EYUSQRilqJk) and [here](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2022/12/12/migranti-il-governo-riattiva-i-respingimenti-sul-confine-sloveno-erano-stati-dichiarati-illegittimi-dal-tribunale-di-roma-nel-2021/6902551/?fbclid=IwAR0D5WSOfhAPO4GpgPDd4BFkvfSJqjrlRUnH32oeVHu_e6_YtLPh20DJakA) .
#### SAR/SEA
### Over 1000 people returned to Libya

MSF denounced the conditions which the people intercepted at sea are being forced to face, since they have been returned to Libya. The 1,000 people found at sea over the past week were, in fact, taken back to Libya. There is no guarantee of safe shelter and asylum. Libya is not safe. The torture to which migrants are subjected in Libya has been known and denounced for years.

#### SPAIN
### Amnesty suspects cover-up of investigations still halted six months after the Melilla massacre

Months pass, but the investigation into the Melilla massacre seems to be stuck, mired in a time bubble. After six months, there is still no clarity on the dynamics and events that led to the deaths of 37 people at the Melilla border on 24 June. Seventy-seven people are still missing. But still the Moroccan as well as the Spanish authorities are denying this. Amnesty international report denounces the suspicion of a cover-up given the uncertain state of the investigation


■■■■■■■■■■■■■■ 
> **[Amnesty International](https://twitter.com/amnesty) @ Twitter Says:** 

> > On 24 June, at least 37 people died after being met by extraordinary violence by Moroccan and Spanish forces when they tried to cross the border. 77 are still missing.

6 months on, there is still no investigation.

Our new report: [amn.st/601431tqg](http://amn.st/601431tqg) 📢 #MelillaNeverAgain 

> **Tweeted at [2022-12-13 10:53:26](https://twitter.com/amnesty/status/1602617686739275776).** 

■■■■■■■■■■■■■■ 


Investigations by the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/extra/z8i55dsu8w/spain-morocco-border) and [Lighthouse Report](https://www.lighthousereports.nl/investigation/reconstructing-the-melilla-massacre/) have shown Spain and Morocco responsibilities for the massacre, but both governments haven’t ensured truth and justice for the victims.


> “(…) Spanish and Moroccan authorities continue to deny any responsibility for the carnage at Melilla. There is a growing mountain of evidence of serious and multiple human rights violations, including the unlawful death and ill-treatment of refugees and migrants and to this day the lack of information as to the identity of the deceased and the fate of the missing”, 





said Agnès Callamard, Amnesty International’s Secretary General.

Not only are the investigations stalled. Governments are hampering families and organisations seeking the truth about the facts, according to Amnesty. Far from being supported, the families of the victims are finding a wall of silence before them.

#### UNITED KINGDOM
### Four lives lost in English Channel

The lives of [four people have been lost](https://twitter.com/Care4Calais/status/1603116402998923264?fbclid=IwAR1yBAxYFyGPQ7ITfxLxMY2m1g0B2lntuJKjZSsRCc-PaH7NdUicrQ-K1lY) and more than 40 others were rescued when a boat capsized in the Channel. The passengers contacted the organisation Utopia 56 who contacted both French and British authorities in order to provide assistance. A fishing vessel also provided help at the scene.

The boat left the French coast early on Wednesday morning and faced difficulties where the French waters meets UK waters. First to assist was a fisherman who [pulled 31 people from the water.](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2022/dec/14/channel-deaths-desperate-call-from-boat-raised-alarm-for-rescue-operation?fbclid=IwAR3QaK2eNjv2pL6O9ZVoY6iAGvd9VN0AUkVrlg4P2tQLxvCfGnDKZT8ZrkU)

British Prime Minister Rishi Sunak is reported to have expressed sympathy for the families of the victims, despite the [Home Secretary stating that no safe and legal routes to claim asylum in the UK will be provided.](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/home-news/channel-deaths-today-migrant-boat-safe-legal-routes-b2245331.html?fbclid=IwAR3tx14R_8pEuWaGj8d1a0snnZw7szdsq_alLO-11-M7fLOiHYAUgsNiCG4)

This comes as even the supposed routes to safety prove impossible to negotiate.
