#### ITALY
### Increase in the number of guardians of unaccompanied foreign minors, but still not enough

In 2022, the number of unaccompanied foreign minors in Italy increased: this increase was also matched by an increase in the number of voluntary guardians. However, the reception system does not yet demonstrate the necessary strength to guarantee appropriate protection for all minors in Italy. For this reason, this article emphasises the need for more voluntary guardians.


[![](https://www.redattoresociale.it/media/mani_di_minori_stranieri_non_accompagnati_msna?s=64)](https://www.redattoresociale.it/article/notiziario/minori_stranieri_non_accompagnati_aumenta_il_numero_dei_tutori_ma_ne_servono_altri?fbclid=IwAR10RNa6TM4PZV66opAwr36ETjlzk8oCA5TB_UszbjEK75CucYRBxBCrv5c)


As explained on the website:


> The path of voluntary guardianship, in the process of being perfected, today consists of several actions that must be supported by the different actors (institutional and non-institutional) in order to facilitate the reception and integration of unaccompanied foreign minors (MSNA) on the territory, as well as coordination between services in a logic of collaboration. 






[![](https://tutelavolontaria.garanteinfanzia.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/tutela-2-hp.jpg)](https://tutelavolontaria.garanteinfanzia.org/index.php/)

#### FRANCE
### The French Senate about to vote on a reform of the immigration bill

The reform is an attempt to limit access to asylum, speeding up the processing of applications and facilitating the repatriation of ‘irregular’ migrants.

The reform, if voted by the Senate, will pass to the National Assembly, which will vote in December.

This reform seems likely to fuel the problems already evident in the current asylum system. While obtaining protection and asylum would be even more complicated with these changes, already vulnerable groups such as minors and very young men remain excluded from the asylum system.


> On October 19, after months spent on the streets, 430 young people were sheltered in emergency accommodation by the prefecture’s services. A few days later, a majority of these young people received a summons in order, according to the prefecture, to submit an asylum application. On their arrival, the trap closes, the document transmitted did not concern an application for asylum, but for a residence permit as an adult.
