### Violence at Sea: Libyan Coast Guard


![(Photo Credit: [Sea Watch](https://twitter.com/seawatch_intl/status/1704155018553655353?fbclid=IwAR3l9iHzZYcijIo13TYu3XbrbuEHDOYOKgUniykMp9AmpRRmLVvl9zci0Qg) )](../assets/6b8d4609ef2c/0*-zFKiXEZBfDFkoyZ)

(Photo Credit: [Sea Watch](https://twitter.com/seawatch_intl/status/1704155018553655353?fbclid=IwAR3l9iHzZYcijIo13TYu3XbrbuEHDOYOKgUniykMp9AmpRRmLVvl9zci0Qg) )

On the 19th, the Sea Watch ship Aurora prevented a pullback by the so-called Libyan coastguard. Forty people were rescued, but survivors stated that four people had drowned during the night. Sea Watch have been told to take the rescued people to Pozzallo in Sicily, 370km away.

Contact was lost with another group in distress. Relatives of the group state that shots were fired at them by a vessel flying the Libyan flag before contact was lost.

#### MALTA
#### One of the ‘El Hiblu three’ missing

Abdul Kader, who was arrested along with two others commandering and steering a boat after they were rescued from the sea, [has been missing since August.](https://timesofmalta.com/articles/view/one-el-hiblu-three-missing-since-early-august-court-told.1056214?fbclid=IwAR21ibG4R8Dwqlxgm0zQeMRLT977GoJZl9nzZWCx5oEhqapoQY8sR3cl4YU) The news came to light when he failed to appear in court on Wednesday this week. Questions as to why it was not brought to the authorities’ attention sooner have been raised. As reported in [this previous News Digest](https://medium.com/are-you-syrious/ays-daily-digest-30-07-2020-trial-against-salvini-and-the-el-hiblu-three-87282b2c483) , the three accused people were just 15, 16 and 19 when they were arrested in 2019 and the trial has dragged on without conclusion since then.
#### SLOVAKIA
#### Rise in migration as election immenent

Slovakia has seen a sudden rise in people entering the country from Hungary. Previously, there had been some border towns where people had been known to pass through, [yet this report states 800](https://www.dw.com/en/slovakia-will-a-rise-in-illegal-migration-sway-voters/a-66887208?fbclid=IwAR28IxyiFSJosKyozsI4oyk_DWeq0b8W6bCnmh9HDnrVSP689FAypAtERok#:~:text=Illegal%20migration%20doesn%27t%20seem%20to%20influence%20voters&text=%22Slovakia%20is%20no%20longer%20the,have%20no%20issues%20with%20them) people were temporarily in the town centre of a small border town. There is specualtion that this is in connection with upcoming elections, with Hungary attempting to sway voters toward right wing views.
#### GREECE
#### 4000 people unlawfully detained

Twenty-two [NGOs have signed a joint statement](https://legalcentrelesvos.org/2023/09/19/joint-statement-unlawful-detention-and-worsening-conditions-over-4000-asylum-seekers-unlawfully-detained-on-samos-and-lesvos/) against the unlawful defacto detention of new arrivals on Samos and Lesvos and the inhumane and degrading treatment they are exposed to through the government’s inability to adequately manage their care.


> 22 organisations, urgently call for an immediate end to the unlawful practice of systematically de facto detaining people upon arrival and awaiting the registration of their asylum requests. The unpreparedness of authorities for the increase in arrivals cannot justify the mass violation of individuals’ right to liberty and freedom from inhuman treatment. 




#### Arson accusations in Evros

The wildfires reported in the Evros region have devasted the lives of people there. Some locals have blamed people on the move for deliberately caused fires, resorting to vigilatism — policing the people themselves. [This long read explores the views of locals.](https://taz.de/Waldbraende-in-Griechenland/!5958241/)


> “I know where their routes are. I stop them, ask them for their names, for papers. If they don’t have any papers, I forcefully ask them to stop.” 





> Christos Kapnas, a member of the public known to the police, has made [500 “arrests”](https://taz-de.translate.goog/Waldbraende-in-Griechenland/!5958241/?_x_tr_sl=en&_x_tr_tl=de&_x_tr_hl=en-US&_x_tr_pto=wapp) of people crossing from Turkey in the last 3 or 4 years.

#### ITALY
#### France states it will support Italy with ‘Illegal Migration’

[Approximately 8,500 people arrived on Lampedusa on 199 boats](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/51963/france-to-support-italy-in-securing-its-border-against-illegal-migrants?fbclid=IwAR0QXLKGLINkih6oVvyfr0gtqGtHiweWUs2AfR9SJnz7KSJyd0FVV22HNqM) between Monday and Wednesday last week. France has said it can take a grand total of 250 while Germany has halted the deal which distributes asylum seekers among member states. The French parliament will discuss a new anti-immigration law this autumn.
#### FRANCE
#### EU demands France abide by law

Despite offers to help Italy, the European Court of Justice has ruled that France cannot turn people away from their border without a sufficient time period elapsing. Organisations have been calling for change for eight years. [More information here.](https://www-lefigaro-fr.translate.goog/international/migrants-la-justice-europeenne-restreint-les-possibilites-de-refoulements-en-france-20230922?fbclid=IwAR3PHZarp8Vqfj5NER1qHO_Am-ASiEnsoTJdLOQtde86di-Ej6uUroeN-SA&_x_tr_sl=fr&_x_tr_tl=en&_x_tr_hl=en-US&_x_tr_pto=wapp)
#### LATVIA
#### Latvia closes one of two border crossings with Belarus

Evika Silina, the new prime minister of Latvia, [has closed one of the country’s border crossings with Belarus](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/51971/latvia-closes-one-of-two-border-crossings-with-belarus-to-stop-irregular-migrants?fbclid=IwAR18Cku0P0OAiczQdnBX8Fid7ARujP5lHmg0I5fVGyjUufWZozGklBxKDEQ) citing ‘illegal migration’, stating that Latvia will finish building their border fence by the end of the year. There have reportedly been nearly 1000 people pushed back in the last week.
#### BELGIUM
#### Men must be housed

Belgian authorities have been attempting to deny housing to men whilst resources are stretched. This has been overturned and ruled as unlawful. [Full details are here.](https://www.euractiv.com/section/politics/news/belgian-court-halts-decision-denying-housing-to-single-male-asylum-seekers/?fbclid=IwAR2T8Nn_ub8t1CmClUlVzgc1twLGzdW426rUHdGsTgzhLxGNYyZHcJFN3mQ)
