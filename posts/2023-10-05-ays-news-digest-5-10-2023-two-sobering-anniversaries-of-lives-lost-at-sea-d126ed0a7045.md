### AYS News Digest 5/10/2023: Two sobering anniversaries of lives lost at sea
#### Dramatic increase of boat arrivals at Aegean islands // Several boats in distress rescued in central Mediterranean // Worrying proposals for EU border management // Repeal of law in Italy proposing to charge failed asylum seekers to avoid detention // Torture, violence and illegal pushbacks on the Bihac-Croatian border // Organisations show solidarity with LGBTQ+ and women migrants against UK Home Secretary comments // Re-implementation of border controls within Schengen // more…
#### FEATURE
#### Two sobering anniversaries of lives lost at sea


![(photo credit via [InfoMigrants](https://twitter.com/InfoMigrants/status/1709594935945527692?s=20) )](../assets/d126ed0a7045/0*1p5gj2TT-SLpIL3b)

(photo credit via [InfoMigrants](https://twitter.com/InfoMigrants/status/1709594935945527692?s=20) )

Groups arrived on Kythira island in Greece to commemorate the one-year anniversary of the shipwreck where, on 5th October last year, at least 15 people were killed trying to reach safety in Europe. In their [post](https://x.com/alarm_phone/status/1709543560884719900?s=20) Alarm Phone calls for financial support for the survivors and relatives saying:


> “One year after the tragedy, we arrived on Kythira to mourn, remember and thank those who did everything to save lives. We come angry at the European borders that killed them and a desire to build a different future in solidarity and without borders.” 





The 10-year anniversary of the 2013 Lampedusa shipwreck, where 366 people lost their lives, was also marked by many, including a protest by the Red Hands anti-racist organisation in Rome. Already this year 2,400 humans have died or gone missing in the Mediterranean.

These two anniversaries appear at a time where we are witnessing increased arrivals at sea in the Aegean, increased violent pushbacks from the Greek Coast Guard and the recent tragedy in June off of Pylos where 650 people drowned. Sea borders have been deadly for so many thousands. How much longer can the EU continue to use them knowingly in a way that causes so much suffering and death?
#### **Sea & SAR**

Within 24 hours of returning to the SAR area of the central Mediterranean, the Louise Michel vessel rescued 53 people from an overcrowded rubber boat. In addition across three separate joint operations between Nadir and Pilotes Volontaires, 125 people in distress were attended to and then handed over to Italian authorities.
#### **Greece**

Aegean Boat Report have released their report for September, highlighting the 966% increase in arrivals on the islands over the past three months, a concerning 170% increase in pushbacks in the Aegean Sea, deteriorating conditions at the so-called “reception centres” due to the massive increase in arrivals and lack of transfers to the mainland, amongst other causes.

#### UK

Stonewall, Europe’s largest LGBTQ+ rights charity along with 245 other organisations have joined together in a coalition to counteract the inflammatory comments made by the Home Secretary Suella Braverman last week regarding this vulnerable group’s eligibility for asylum.


> “We note that your Government’s own statistics suggest that only 2% of all asylum claims made in the UK in 2022 included sexual orientation as a reason for seeking protection. We regret that the Home Secretary intentionally singled out this small minority of people for reasons that have nothing to do with genuine concern or respect for international law, refugees or their protection.” 





The [letter](https://www.stonewall.org.uk/sites/default/files/letter_to_rt_hon_rishi_sunak_pm_respect_refugee_lives.pdf) points out not only the persecution that LGBTQ+ people and women encounter in many of their home countries, but also the disclosures that they are often forced to make in order to claim asylum in the UK and the “evidence” that they have to provide of their sexuality, both of which can be incredibly humiliating and retraumatising.

Care4Calais have also [commented](https://web.facebook.com/care4calais/posts/pfbid022hX71F9jibjPgN8z96XSX32Vr2vjwrrXCQjx4YPVHt8XdRo5miGf9mv6RpzfXux4l) on Braverman’s speech that was given at the Conservative Party conference on 3rd October, saying it was ‘verging on the language used by the far-right and steeped in the so-called “culture wars”.’ They remind us that only 7% of refugees claiming asylum in Europe came to the UK.
#### EU

Further details are emerging concerning the EU’s new Pact on Asylum and Migration. [First proposed in 2020](https://www.opendemocracy.net/en/can-europe-make-it/the-eus-pact-against-migration-part-one/) , the newly negotiated Crisis Regulation section appears to allow EU Member states to apply even stricter measures at the border in times of crisis. The ECRE are [expressing alarm](https://x.com/ecre/status/1709575169474171121?s=20) at these proposed measures which could lead to large-scale confinement and the decrease in the quality of the asylum procedure, both of which are a risk to people’s rights to access a fair asylum system. [The Left in the European Parliament](https://twitter.com/Left_EU/status/1709595192347521459?s=20&fbclid=IwAR2BLNPqQz5VVb3kUi2XulL6WkvSkeC1NZmkAQ7bNXfL8DTt2YfYZKXFr-Y) state:


> “The agreement reached by EU countries on the crisis regulation is paving the way for the abolition of the right to asylum in Europe.” 




#### Italy

The call of the Italian government [to force rejected asylum applicants to pay almost](https://medium.com/are-you-syrious/ays-news-digest-26-09-23-rejected-asylum-applicants-must-pay-4938-euro-to-avoid-detention-in-italy-31561cdabf8) € [5000](https://medium.com/are-you-syrious/ays-news-digest-26-09-23-rejected-asylum-applicants-must-pay-4938-euro-to-avoid-detention-in-italy-31561cdabf8) not to be detained has been repealed in a court ruling in Catania, Sicily. The judge found the decree “ [illegal in several parts.”](https://www.faz.net/aktuell/politik/ausland/gericht-in-catania-setzt-italienisches-asyldekret-ausser-kraft-19213790.html) The Interior Minister has said that the government will appeal the ruling.
#### Slovakia

Slovakia has announced [it will reintroduce controls along its 644km border with Hungary](https://www.infomigrants.net/fr/post/52318/la-slovaquie-retablit-a-son-tour-des-controles-aux-frontieres?fbclid=IwAR1YKzUSIo2pptjutTHH_TcP3sTlhv-FLjDcbifc--DBPuFZbytWdquejUc) in order to repel migrants. Starting from 5th October this will last at least 10 days. Already around 500 soldiers have been deployed to this border area until the end of the year where, between January and September, around 39,000 people have tried to cross its border.

Poland, Austria and Czechia have already taken similar decisions, along with Germany who last week announced re-establishing border controls along their border with Poland and Czechia. As these areas are all within the supposedly border-free Schengen zone, these are supposed to be measures taken only in exceptional circumstances and Brussels must be notified in advance of their implementation.
#### Serbia

Poor conditions, a fallible reception system and fertile ground for corruption make Serbia a difficult place to be in for people on the move. With a general rise in racism, less and less support to turn to, and a lack of safe and legal routes, people still turn mostly to dangerous and unreliable smuggling systems in order to move. According to a report by Global Initiative ( [https://riskbulletins.globalinitiative.net/.../01...](https://riskbulletins.globalinitiative.net/see-obs-016/01-increasing-migratory-pressures-in-serbia-have-led-to-conflict.html?fbclid=IwAR2s_-Oc-x0RZcDVK2vvfKJiQVvElJfnicp36QqR8oFXViLpj-rM1_uyTqE) ) , ‘smuggling groups active in Serbia are transnational, well-organised, flexible and dangerous’, InfoPark mentions in their monthly newsletter. The report suggests that it usually costs approximately €3,000 for one person to be smuggled from the Horgoš area into Hungary, however, another article ( [https://www.infomigrants.net/.../afghan-killed-in...](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.infomigrants.net%2Fen%2Fpost%2F50650%2Fafghan-killed-in-shootout-near-serbian-border-town%3Ffbclid%3DIwAR3EGuNgmo3QwSdbDBNMwSsAcfLiIzUqIj2iTssc2GIziwWjXrSx-7y22B0&h=AT3EESoidf5AxHY9PD3qTfpfI9uxvGePwUCTuRLVdrsQzkizMiSNRU9NpfnuHPHhlXtdGrM7j7gUol9322Ne_MU_txwErL95UwrDo9KmiOULcL_3beelqsVj8V4XC21Ahuq2mcQ&__tn__=R]-R&c[0]=AT3TwhBI2fgEe_oCvqMgSdSgwTK-2ZKPjYgGMtoNDGyzxWFBEYHnOOE2Aa6PNJB2hE45LydwTCw0DqrFBHZPXlJUg3laSZ35cUSOrGZP67tFag_qW03CSvxzhLHxkDvbEPd6-D3j9_GpicG3rHUQIEKTBqyIAsyrbCokZCkujrUA94XfBUfgCIamfKZPS_aasxCfGyoML-xqDJFv) ) states that individuals may have paid up to €10,000 from the beginning of their journey to different agents in order to reach the external border of the EU.

One of the cases of the InfoPark team documents kidnapping by the smugglers:

“At the end of the month, an individual from Lebanon spoke to Info Park to report that he had been kidnapped by a group of Syrian smugglers from Dair ez-Zor, and held at an unknown location in Belgrade. The smugglers had taken all his belongings, including his phone and a €2000 transfer code, which they then used to steal the money. The man said that five other men and women were imprisoned at the same location, but he alone had managed to escape through a window. The gang is apparently directly connected to an armed Syrian group, also from Dair ez-Zor, which is known to kidnap migrants as soon as they cross the Serbian-Bulgarian border. Info Park reported the case through contacting a police officer from the Department of Foreigners.”

Arrivals in Belgrade have also continued since August, with 2368 new arrivals recorded in September, constituting the second highest number recorded in 2023.
#### Slovenia

A [statement by Pravna mreža za varstvo demokracije](https://pravna-mreza.si/arhiv-blog/policija-bi-morala-preganjati-sovrazni-govor-ne-pa-ga-sama-razpihovati/?fbclid=IwAR1YKzUSIo2pptjutTHH_TcP3sTlhv-FLjDcbifc--DBPuFZbytWdquejUc) in Slovenia has been released criticising the behaviour of the Slovenian police force following revelations that Slovenia does not have the capacity to accommodate applicants for international protection. Police statements from a press conference in September were analysed to show that the police publicly encouraged and incited hatred, violence or intolerance against asylum seekers, and the action was done in a way that could jeopardize or disrupt public order and peace.

“What political solution can be found for migrants from North Africa — apart from the inadequate status quo that offers only fast-track negative asylum applications, deportations, criminalization, exploitative jobs and life without documents and rights on national and European levels?” — This was the main question of the panel and event organised in Ljubljana on Monday by our fellow activists in Slovenia, InfoKolpa, who later concluded:

“There is no Geneva Convention for class war.

An insightful conversation with Moroccan asylum seekers yesterday evening in Atrij ZRC about the situation in Morocco that causes people to migrate. It is a story of neoliberal destruction of the public sphere that affects public healthcare, schools and infrastructure and of the highjacking of the state and its resources by the monarchy and its oligarchs.

At the same time, the outdated European asylum regime that is based on the Geneva Convention excludes high numbers of migrants coming to Europe from so-called safe countries like Morocco — so, other inclusive political solutions must be imagined and called for!”
#### Bosnia and Herzegovina/Croatia

Solidarians at No Name Kitchen have shared a [chilling testimony](https://web.facebook.com/NoNameKitchenBelgrade/posts/pfbid0qxUbYG2bD7WCrBbMJKTquo3eNDirwB492hVmLWXDYips9CRV6REqj7xSDzJMXRLvl) of migrants who were trying to cross the border to continue their migration. The men, ten from Afghanistan and two from India, were tracked down by drones, tortured, beaten and robbed by Croatian police before being pushed back to Bihac.
