#### PALESTINE
#### Israel declares war on the Gaza strip

Following an unprecedented attack by Hamas on Saturday, Israel has launched a bombing campaign in the Gaza Strip, killing more than 700 Palestinians. On Monday, it announced a “total blockade” of the Gaza Strip, stopping the supply of food, fuel and other essential commodities in an act that under international law amounts to a war crime.
