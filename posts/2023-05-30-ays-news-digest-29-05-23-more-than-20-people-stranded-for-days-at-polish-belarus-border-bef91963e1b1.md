#### FEATURE
### Not a step forward, not a step back: people stranded at the Polish-Belarusian border wait in inhuman conditions


> “For several days, a group of more than 20 people from Syria and Iraq have been trapped at the barrier on the Polish-Belarusian border. 11 of them are children, some as young as 2 years old. They are asking to be allowed to cross into Poland and apply for asylum” 




reported No Borders Team in their telegram channel

Although the group is already on Polish territory, no-one has been able to ask for asylum. In fact, the wall between Poland and Belarus “was built, depending on the location, at a distance of one to three meters from the real border line, which allows free movement on the territory of the Republic without overcoming a physical barrier”, as explained by [Grupa Granica](https://www.facebook.com/grupagranica/posts/pfbid02mvgdi2t8AijT45j86ERgcPi1Dkc2SasX6BLLUhL19DaFtx6X1KBXdErBXZMSaZql) . A metal fence about [190 km long](https://twitter.com/InfoMigrants/status/1663144439676534786?s=20&fbclid=IwAR0FJOz7nVkW35P9VculJ0zWdQrDAP4fJwfdnxLXU3BgW_V3wkinBU5dpUY) was erected last year by the Polish authorities in order to hinder people seeking safety from entering the country from Belarus.

The wall has made the route harder and more dangerous. People arriving from Asia and Africa have no choice but to risk their lives. The majority of people in the stranded group are from Iraq, Syria and Congo.

Grupa Granica said:


> “Most of the group are Syrians from the northern areas of the country. Their livelihoods were destroyed by a massive earthquake earlier this year. The others are Kurds from Iraq, fleeing persecution. In their countries of origin, they basically have nothing to go back to” 





Although they have repeatedly asked for protection, they are receiving no response by the Border Guard officers present on the site. On the other side, there is also no way back. The Belarusian authorities are preventing them from re-entering Belarusian territory, threatening them with dogs.

Meanwhile, people with medical needs are suffering more and more and the inhumane conditions are endangering the health of all, including children.

In fact, the main result of the wall and border controls is to endanger people’s lives, more than to prevent them from crossing. Within the stranded group there is a woman who already tried to cross last November. She fell from the fence and broke both legs.

If the fence itself is hurting people, border guards and pushbacks are also making the situation worse.


![A man seeking protection was beaten by polish authorities at the border. Via [Grupa Granica](https://www.facebook.com/grupagranica)](../assets/bef91963e1b1/1*PWTZyTsv0snCj6xO9FCLQg.jpeg)

A man seeking protection was beaten by polish authorities at the border. Via [Grupa Granica](https://www.facebook.com/grupagranica)

Recently, Grupa Granica reported evidence of Polish border guards’ violence. In the picture above, a man shows his back after being beaten by the Polish authorities. [Grupa Granica](https://www.facebook.com/grupagranica/posts/pfbid02AdfD3THLcsRB67wUNKxtaALYtyGxr9WH73t6g3p4k1UnNTVxNRvTtNp4vQ46a5Yhl) added:


> We receive information about many similar situations, as well as about theft and destruction of property (including phones) of refugees. People experiencing violence are often pushed back to Belarus, where they face additional danger from the Belarusian border guards. 





The violence at the border is systematic. And it often ends up killing people. Last week on more person died at the border between Poland and Belarus. Not the first, not the second, not the third person killed by this border. But the 45th.

#### SEA/SAR
### Rescues at sea over the previous days

Sea rescues continue thanks to the continuous activity of multiple NGOs.

Last Sunday, MSF SEA confirmed that over 600 people had finally safely boarded the GeoBarents.


■■■■■■■■■■■■■■ 
> **[MSF Sea](https://twitter.com/MSF_Sea) @ Twitter Says:** 

> > ‼️  UPDATE

After everyone has safely boarded the #GeoBarents and the situation has calmed down, we confirm that we have a total of 606 survivors on board, including 11 women and 151 minors. 

➡️ Among the minors, 20 children are under 13 years old, including very young babies.

Numerous organisations are active on a daily basis to save people’s lives at sea, but their rescue activities are hampered. One way to make disembarkation more difficult is to assign distant ports, endangering the health of the people on board.

This has been denounced by Sea-Eye in a tweet:


> The distance to the safe haven assigned to the [#SEAEYE4](https://twitter.com/hashtag/SEAEYE4?src=hashtag_click) of Italy is more than three times the distance to the nearest port, Pozzallo. The SEA-EYE 4 will probably need four and a half days for the more than 1,300-kilometer route

#### NORTH MACEDONIA
### Expression of Concern to Frontex and MEPs regarding shooting at the Greece/North Macedonia border released by BVMN and Second Tree

Following the murder of Fatmata, shot and killed by a North Macedonian police officer on 19th April 2023, Border Violence Monitoring Network (BVMN) and Second Tree have released an expression of Concern to Frontex and MEPs regarding the shooting at the Greece/North Macedonia border.


[![](https://borderviolence.eu/uploads/report/image/5342/fatmata-cover(2).png)](https://borderviolence.eu/reports/expression-of-concern-to-frontex-and-meps-regarding-shooting-at-the-greece-north-macedonia-border/?fbclid=IwAR3F36Gk3Xrh8GzdQ4t3kVpvG5AIOpvXv4OhVaNjJ_y9OXIIINEQD_qVNuU)



> “BVMN and Second Tree call on MEPs to support the wishes of Abu Bakar and the rest of Fatmata’s family: for a full, prompt investigation and a fair trial, and acknowledgement that the fundamental rights situation in North Macedonia is not in line with international and Union human rights law”
