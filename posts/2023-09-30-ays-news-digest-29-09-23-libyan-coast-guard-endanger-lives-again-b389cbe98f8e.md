#### FEATURE: Libyan Coast Guard endanger lives (again)

A boat carrying 50 people was circled and rammed by the Libyan Coast Guard until the vessel overturned. [The events were witnessed and filmed by Sea-Watch’s plane, Seabird](https://x.com/seawatch_intl/status/1707803438208196954?s=20) . The video clearly shows the violent intent of the intervention and its aftermath as people struggle for their lives.

[The Louise Michel ship, which had previously rescued 58 people](https://x.com/MVLouiseMichel/status/1707742907443093554?s=20) from another overcrowded rubber boat, responded to the distress call and continued to seas approximately 48 km north of the Libyan city of Zuwara where the 50 people were in distress both in the sea and aboard their sinking dingy.

The Louise Michel’s request that survivors of this incident be taken on board their ship was denied by the Libyan Coast Guard. The survivors appear to have been pulled back to Libya. It remains unclear if anybody drowned as a result. When the Louise Michel searched the area they found no bodies and no remaining survivors.


> The so-called Libyan coastguard, funded and armed by Italy and Europe, consists of ruthless criminals. The time for European states to stop their support for human rights violations is now! [_Sea-Watch International_](https://twitter.com/seawatch_intl/status/1707803438208196954) 




#### GREECE
#### 35 people disappear into thin air off Samos

On the morning of the 28th, [Alarm Phone was alerted by relatives](https://twitter.com/alarm_phone/status/1707397248831668455) that 35 people including nine children were in distress north of Samos, Greece and that the Hellenic Coast Guard was present. Yet communication was quickly lost with the group and the Coast Guard stated that they had found no one in the area. Another pushback?
#### ITALY
#### New centre opened for people from ‘safe’ countries

[Italy has opened a new centre](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/52183/italy-opens-first-center-for-asylum-seekers-from-safe-countries#) for people coming from countries it considers safe in the Sicilian port city of Pozzallo. It is unclear whether this centre will be opened or closed, but it remains likely that being placed there will negatively impact a person’s ability to claim asylum.
#### BULGARIA
#### Bodies abandoned at border

A [new report](https://www.meltingpot.org/en/2023/09/bulgaria-rosenovo-does-not-exist/) on Melting Pot Europa documents the experience of the [Collettivo Rotte Balcaniche Alto Vicentino](https://web.facebook.com/profile.php?id=100078755275162&_rdc=1&_rdr) in their search for the body of Saif al-Ahmad, a 22-year-old man from Syria who died in Bulgaria on his journey from Turkey into Europe.

The collective started their search on the evening of 26th August, having received information from the [Consolidated Rescue Group](https://www.crg-group.net/) .

The Collettivo members repeatedly contacted the Bulgarian emergency services before and after locating the body themselves. It seems apparent from the report that, despite this, no real action was taken to try to locate or recover the body by Bulgarian authorities and it was only the persistence of the Colletivo that meant that eventually Saif’s body was recovered.

The team report their challenging and confusing communication with Bulgarian rescue services and border police. Through their numerous phone calls and interactions, the Bulgarian authorities seemed to actively discourage the group from continuing their quest to recover Saif’s body from the woods where he had died. They say now their focus is to try and repatriate Saif’s body to his family.


> What causes the death of those who cross them are not the woods but the borders, those who argue for their necessity and those who defend them with the teeth of dogs and loaded guns. 




#### LATVIAN-BELARUS BORDER
#### Two vulnerable women pushed back to Belarus from Latvian border

[Sienos Grupè report](https://web.facebook.com/sienosgrupe) that the two women had been travelling with a larger group who had left them behind when one of them injured her leg and could no longer walk. After having called the authorities, the two Somali women received basic medical aid, food and water at a Latvian border post before being pushed back into Belarus.

Two days later, the women tried again to get to Latvia through the forest. Once again they did not succeed and eventually were assisted by some villagers who took them to a nearby town. Officers were contacted and they were then taken to Minsk.

Civil Society actors were unable to intervene due to fear of criminalization.
#### GERMANY
#### Germany to fund NGO rescue operations in the Med

The German Foreign Minister, Annalena Baerbock, has announced that [Germany will provide funding to NGOs carrying out search and rescue](https://www.liberal.gr/diethni-themata/metanasteytiko-berolino-tha-enishysei-tis-mko-poy-drastiriopoioyntai-sti-mesogeio) operations in the Mediterranean. Italy’s prime minister, Giorgia Meloni, is reportedly unhappy about this development.
