#### FEATURE: Two weeks, three shipwrecks, 109 dead, 37 missing

[On 27th February](https://www.ansa.it/english/news/general_news/2023/03/09/support-to-victims-families-ensured-crotone-prefecture_a12a2c68-e9fa-4280-ac56-b008d4ce4163.html) the world was informed of a deadly shipwreck off the coast of Crotone, Italy. Since then [it has come to light that authorities including Frontex and the Italian Search and Rescue services were all well informed](https://www.ft.com/content/74bfeddd-eea5-4f69-bd05-c33fcebba8d6) of the boat long before 72 people drowned. Despite this, both governments and responsible agencies expressed their ‘profound grief’ that this tragedy had taken place while hurling blame and insults across the Mediterranean. The Italian government even made a momentary immigration U-turn and blamed the Greek pushback policy for the rise of boats taking the Turkey-Italy route. Strange days indeed.

Unfortunately, but predictably, nothing was learnt from the Crotone catastrophe. [Early on 11th March the Turkish coastguard](https://m.facebook.com/story.php?story_fbid=pfbid02YfovhnY16onNQCFLsmZQRSPCwRFNuL6yx1yzHdT9oXT3UhSKuqPNqmcQE6YtJmsal&id=100064851724634) found a semi-submerged boat near the Greek island of Farmakonisi after which the bodies of seven people were found. Five people from the same boat, however, were discovered alive on the island, which is Greek territory, suggesting that the group may have been subject to a pushback.

(Of these 47 people, only 17 survived. Photo Credit: [Sea-Watch International](https://twitter.com/seawatch_intl) )

[On 12th March, 30 more people were killed](https://alarmphone.org/en/2023/03/12/30-people-die-due-to-non-assistance-by-the-italian-authorities/?fbclid=IwAR3-Q7QWg5WaVBUPzkhtByROY13AkroMh0PXAZMEtxzdr5prCgsA6zvxxi4) by an active non-assistance policy. Neither Italy nor Libya would take charge of the rescue and again responsibility was taken by no-one. [The Libyan Rescue Centre told Sea Watch that they would not send a ship](https://twitter.com/seawatch_intl/status/1634604262914179072/photo/1) , as they did not have any available. The Italian MRCC hung up on them.

> Why, after the deadly shipwreck of Crotone and the innumerable deaths and disappearances in the Mediterranean Sea over the last years, is the EU still militarising its borders, deterring people on the move, and letting thousands drown? — [_Alarm Phone_](https://alarmphone.org/en/2023/03/12/30-people-die-due-to-non-assistance-by-the-italian-authorities/?fbclid=IwAR3-Q7QWg5WaVBUPzkhtByROY13AkroMh0PXAZMEtxzdr5prCgsA6zvxxi4) 





One thing is abundantly clear, removing search and rescue vessels from the Mediterranean through a combination of criminalization and obscure orders to find safe port only at several days distance, does not discourage people from making the journey, it only makes the mortality rate of these journeys higher. This is an undeniable fact, as obvious to policy makers as it is to people on the move and activists on the ground. Yet these policies are continued despite the risk, knowingly, leading to the deaths of 109 people in just two weeks. Everyday more lives are put at risk and each death is used as a deterrent by governments.

[Prevention through deterrence as a policy was brought into effect at the US-Mexican border in 1994](https://actionnetwork.org/letters/tell-congress-end-prevention-through-deterrence) and utilizes dangerous landscapes to deter migration. This policy has been widely documented and criticized.


> By pushing traffic into remote and hostile terrain, the \[US Border\] Agency speculated that border crossers would now find themselves “in mortal danger” when attempting to enter the US without authorization. The increased danger was intended to then deter other people from considering the journey, with the overall goal of preventing migration. — [_Action Network_](https://actionnetwork.org/letters/tell-congress-end-prevention-through-deterrence) 





Meanwhile, at the same time, [humanitarian aid at the US-Mexican border, which could actively prevent people dying during their journey by providing access to water for example, was criminalized](https://latino.ucla.edu/research/criminalizing-humanitarian-aid-at-the-u-s-mexico-border/) . This has lead to thousands of deaths. Sound familiar?

In 2021 it was revealed that [at least 2000 deaths could be directly related to pushbacks from the EU](https://www.theguardian.com/global-development/2021/may/05/revealed-2000-refugee-deaths-linked-to-eu-pushbacks) . The UK has taken up a similar approach to small boat crossings in the channel.


> The policies referenced above — which aim also to prevent migration through deterrence — have effectively empowered the British government to indirectly determine who may live and who may die. At the same time, the state has produced a narrative about ‘protecting lives’ and ‘preventing tragedy’ in order to frame these same policies as humanitarian measures. These policies push people into lorries without oxygen and onto overcrowded dinghies, exposing them to death while displacing responsibility onto smuggling groups. 





> Ensuing deaths are, in turn, characterised as ‘tragedies’ and evidence of the danger of the Channel or of lorries themselves. Death is misidentified as a risk inevitably taken by those who choose to cross irregularly. In reality, however, there are no other routes from which to choose. Through this repeated portrayal, border deaths are used to justify the policies that produce them in the first place. — [_Refugee Rights Europe_](https://refugee-rights.eu/2021/08/20/representing-channel-deaths-how-the-state-displaces-its-responsibility-through-strategic-narratives/) 




#### ITALY
#### 18 activists acquitted

[18 left-wing activists have been acquitted in Turin](https://www.infomigrants.net/en/post/47434/italy-judge-acquits-18-turin-activists-who-helped-migrants) after they were accused of trespassing on public property because evidence showed that the self managed space they were involved in provided necessary support to people on the move.

The former roadman’s house in Oulx was occupied on 9 December 2018, and supported people on the Ventimiglia to Alta Valle di Susa route until March 2021. The often freezing conditions along this route are known to have taken at least 30 people’s lives since 2014.
#### UK
#### Ukrainians to move off cruise ship

In August 2022, the Scottish Government hired a second cruise ship and docked it near Glasgow. The Ukrainians housed there will be moved to land-based accommodation by 31st March. The cruise ship, MS Ambition, is a part of a £100 million deal between that Scottish Government and Corporate Travel Management. The disparity between the welcome Ukrainian refugees have received and the illegalization and racist rhetoric that refugees from other countries face has been described in the media as a [state-imposed hierarchy of refugees](https://www.newstatesman.com/the-weekend-report/2023/02/ukrainians-cruise-ship-hierarchy-refugees) .

There is a [call by Refuweegee to gather suitcases](https://twitter.com/Refuweegee/status/1634252819355279360) to support people to move their belongings to new accommodation.
#### Football star nearly lost his job for supporting refugees

One week ago ex-footballer, Gary Lineker, was taken off his regular slot on BBC TV for openly comparing UK Home Office immigration policy to Nazi Germany. He has now been reinstated and will be broadcasting again from Saturday.

[“A final thought: however difficult the last few days have been, it simply doesn’t compare to having to flee your home from persecution or war to seek refuge in a land far away. It’s heartwarming to have seen the empathy towards their plight from so many of you. 3/4”](https://twitter.com/GaryLineker/status/1635221458661285889)

Demonstrations against the bill continue and you can find an updated list of anti-hate demos here — [Fighting hate with love, one town at a time — Care4Calais](https://care4calais.org/news/anti-hate-rallies/?fbclid=IwAR30cxIKU8OofE8jXCB0K5KJHluc7UgKSqN_wfh26fl0M5sCaEhYkJb6zW4) .
